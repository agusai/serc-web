Club President

"Founded in 2024 with fewer than 15 players, we began as a small group with a big dream. Today, we are more than 60 players strong. From a grassroots start, we have grown into a committed rugby community built on passion and development. Our goal is simple — to use rugby to shape boys and girls into individuals with strong values and discipline, both on and off the field."
